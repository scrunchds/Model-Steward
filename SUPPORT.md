# Support

Community support is provided on a best-effort basis through repository discussions or appropriately scoped issues once the public repository is available.

Do not use public support channels for vulnerabilities, credentials, private source code, customer information, or other sensitive material. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for security reports.

Commercial support, deployment assistance, redistribution, paid hosting, and business use require a separate commercial agreement. See [`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md).
