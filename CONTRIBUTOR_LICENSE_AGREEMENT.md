# Contributor Licensing Policy

Model Steward is dual-licensed for noncommercial and commercial use. To preserve the ability to offer commercial licenses, outside contributions cannot be accepted until the project owner has adopted a contributor license agreement (CLA) workflow.

Until then:

- bug reports, design feedback, and test cases are welcome;
- do not submit substantive code contributions unless the owner has explicitly provided and accepted a CLA;
- contributors retain copyright in their work unless a signed agreement states otherwise;
- merely opening an issue does not transfer copyright.

Before public contribution intake, the owner should obtain legal review and deploy an explicit CLA covering copyright and patent permissions, including the right to sublicense contributions under both source-available and commercial terms.
